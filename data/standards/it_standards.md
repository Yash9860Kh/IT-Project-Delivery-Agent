## 9. DPDP Act 2023 Awareness

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 governs the processing of digital personal data in India and emphasizes lawful processing, consent or legitimate use, data protection, and accountability.

### Key Principles
- Lawful use of digital personal data
- Consent-based processing where required
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimization
- Accuracy
- Storage limitation
- Security safeguards
- Accountability

### Role in Project
This helps the AI agent provide responsible guidance when project data includes personal or sensitive digital information.

## 10. Information Technology Act 2000 Awareness

The Information Technology Act, 2000 provides legal recognition to electronic records and digital communication in India.

### Key Relevance

- Support for digital communication and electronic governance
- Recognition of secure digital practices
- Basis for responsible digital operations
